Why It Matters

Most Resumes Are Never Read by a Person

Most employers run applications through an applicant tracking system before a recruiter sees them. A resume that doesn't use the language of the job, or that the software can't read, gets filtered out quietly. The ones that pass then get a quick skim from a busy hiring manager.

So your resume has two audiences, a machine and a human, and it has to work for both. That's hard to do for yourself, and almost impossible to do with one generic resume sent everywhere.

Common Questions

Resume Writing FAQ

How much does professional resume writing cost?

$199 for a resume rewritten for one specific job, delivered within 5 working days. A cover letter for the same job is $99.

Is my resume written by a person or by AI?

By a person. Your resume is rewritten by a member of our team for the job you're going for, and checked by a person before you send it.

Why tailor a resume to one job instead of writing one for everything?

Every job posting uses its own language, and screening software looks for that language. One general resume matches every job a little and none of them well. A resume written for the job in front of you is the one most likely to reach a person.

Will you add skills or experience I don't have?

No. We rewrite your real experience so it reads at its strongest for the role. Anything on your resume should be something you can talk about confidently in the interview.
