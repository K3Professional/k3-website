Our Story

Built for People, Not Just Resumes

K3 Professional Services began with a frustration shared by so many job seekers: the modern career search has been reduced to algorithms, automated filters, and one-size-fits-all templates. Somewhere along the way, the person behind the resume got lost.

We set out to bring the human back. K3 pairs real coaches with real people — listening to your story, challenging your thinking, and building a strategy that fits your life, not a generic playbook. From your first consultation to the day you accept an offer, you work with people who know your name and your goals.

Alongside our coaching, we built Resumaire — a self-service career tool for the moments you want to move on your own. Same family, different needs: the human partner when you want guidance, the smart tool when you want speed.
