Our Approach

Practice With the People Who Make the Decisions

Most interview advice is a list of questions to read on your own. That helps you know what's coming, but it doesn't tell you how your answer sounds to the person deciding whether to hire you.

Our founder has spent more than 15 years conducting interviews and making hiring decisions, and our COO has spent more than a decade coaching professionals through real-time feedback. A mock interview with us is practice with that experience on the other side of the table.

We focus on what actually changes the outcome: telling your story clearly, answering behavioral questions with a real example and a real result, and knowing the employer well enough to make your answers specific.
