Common Questions

Career Coaching FAQ

Do you only work with clients in Florida?

No. K3 Professional Services is based in Florida and works with clients nationwide. Every session is virtual, so you can work with us from anywhere in the United States.

How much does professional resume writing cost?

A resume rewritten by a person and tailored to one specific job is $199, delivered within 5 working days. A cover letter for one job is $99.

What happens in a career coaching strategy session?

The $249 Strategy Session is a one-hour, one-on-one session to plan your next move: career direction, a focused job-search plan, and recommendations on the certifications and courses worth your time. You leave with written notes and a clear plan.

Do you offer mock interviews and interview coaching?

Yes. A $199 mock interview is a real interview for the job you are going for, followed by written feedback on what landed and what to rehearse. A $99 interview cheat sheet researches one employer and the questions they are likely to ask.

Can you handle my whole job search for me?

Yes. Concierge is a done-for-you service for $1,497, one time. It includes a live strategy call with our team, openings in your field with every employer researched by hand, and a resume and cover letter tailored to each opening, all delivered within your first two weeks.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Everyone can book one free 15-minute discovery call. We learn about your goals and point you to the right next step. Strategy, resume reviews, and coaching are paid services.

Are there free career tools I can try first?

Yes. The free career assessment takes about 15 minutes and suggests five to eight career directions that fit you. The free ATS resume check shows how applicant tracking systems score your resume. Neither needs an account.

How do I book and pay for a service?

All K3 services are booked and paid securely through Resumaire, our career platform. Every service includes 7 days of full access to Resumaire’s career tools.
