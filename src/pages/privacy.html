Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how K3 Professional Services, LLC (“K3,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit k3professionalservices.com or work with us. It is a plain-language explanation, not legal advice. We aim to be clear about what we collect and why.

1. Information We Collect

Information you send us. This website has no user accounts and does not store anything you type into it. When you use one of our forms (the consultation request, the testimonial form, or the blog topic suggestion form), your device opens your own email app with your answers filled in, addressed to general@k3professionalservices.com. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email. When you email or call us, we receive whatever you include, such as your name, contact details, career goals, and work history.

Information you share while working with us. To provide career coaching, resume and LinkedIn strategy, interview preparation, job-search planning, and concierge services, we may collect your name, contact information, location, career goals, work history, and documents you choose to share, such as a current resume or a job description. We only ask for what we need to deliver the service you requested.

Appointment bookings. When you book a call through our booking page, the details you enter (such as your name, email address, phone number, and chosen time) are collected by Microsoft Bookings and shared with us so we can hold the appointment.

Technical information. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically receives basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, as part of delivering the site. We do not use this to identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions and consultation requests.
  • Schedule and hold consultations and coaching sessions.
  • Prepare resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, and interview materials on your behalf.
  • Communicate with you about the services you have requested.
  • Review the testimonials and blog topic suggestions you send us.
  • Keep records needed for billing, taxes, and our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not add you to a marketing mailing list without your permission.

3. Email and Forms

Our forms work through your own email app, so your message travels like any other email you send. Our business email is provided by Microsoft, and messages you send us are stored in our Microsoft email account. If you would rather not use the forms, you can reach us directly by email or by phone at 321-966-4940.

4. Testimonials and Blog Topic Suggestions

Testimonials and topic suggestions you send us are kept private until our team reviews them. A testimonial is only published on our website or in our marketing if you gave us permission when you submitted it, and only with the name and role you provided. Your email address is never published. Topic suggestions are used to plan future articles and are not shared publicly.

5. Resumaire

Resumaire is the career platform from K3 Professional Services, LLC, at resumaire.com. The free career assessment, the free ATS resume check, Resumaire’s self-service tools and plans, and the booking and payment of K3 services all take place on Resumaire, not on this website. Information you provide there is handled under Resumaire’s Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

We use a small number of outside services to run this website and our business:

  • Netlify hosts this website and delivers its pages.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used on the site. Your browser loads them from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Microsoft provides our business email and our appointment booking page.

This website also links to other websites, including our social media profiles and the course and certification providers on our Certifications page. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information. Please review their policies before sharing personal details with them.

7. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Sites we link to, such as Resumaire, our booking page, and our social media profiles, may use their own cookies under their own policies.

8. Data Protection and Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access. Coaching notes and documents you share with us are stored securely, and access is limited to the K3 team members and trusted specialists directly supporting your engagement. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

9. Information Sharing

We share your information only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as those listed in section 6, and only as needed for them to do so.
  • With your explicit consent, such as when we submit applications or contact recruiters on your behalf as part of a concierge engagement.
  • When required by law.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information for as long as we need it to provide our services and meet our business and legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

11. Your Choices

  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us.
  • You can choose not to provide certain information, though this may limit the services we can offer.
  • You can withdraw permission to publish a testimonial at any time, and we will remove it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date above shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at general@k3professionalservices.com or 321-966-4940.
