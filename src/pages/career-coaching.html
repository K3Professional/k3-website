Common Questions

Career Coaching FAQ

What does a career coach actually do?

A career coach helps you work out where you're going and how to get there: clarifying your direction, building a job-search plan, strengthening how you present yourself, and keeping you accountable. At K3, that coaching comes from people who have spent years on the hiring side.

How much does career coaching cost?

A one-hour Strategy Session is $249, including written notes and a clear plan. If you'd rather we handle the search for you, Concierge is $1,497, one time. Your first 15-minute discovery call is free.

Can a career coach help me change careers?

Yes. Career changers are one of the groups we work with most. We help you identify the skills that carry over, choose a credible next step, and decide which certifications or courses are worth it.

Is coaching done online?

Yes. All of our coaching is virtual, so we work with clients across Florida and nationwide.

What should I do before my first session?

Take the free career assessment if you're unsure of your direction. It takes about 15 minutes, and bringing your results gives your coach a head start.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. Everyone can book one free 15-minute discovery call. We learn about your goals and point you to the right next step.
